The Problem

Postpartum hemorrhage doesn't wait for the right equipment.

PPH is the leading cause of maternal death

Postpartum hemorrhage (PPH) is the leading cause of maternal mortality worldwide. Uterine suction tamponade (UST) is an emerging, effective treatment: a soft device is placed in the uterus and connected to suction, applying gentle negative pressure that helps the uterus collapse and contract — compressing bleeding vessels from the inside.

Standard suction depends on fixed infrastructure

UST is typically delivered through hospital wall suction. That works well in a fully-equipped OR — but it leaves care teams without options in labor & delivery rooms, ambulances, rural facilities, and disaster or conflict settings where wall suction isn't available or reliable.

The Product

VATARA: portable suction, purpose-built for UST.

A battery-powered pump paired with a single-use consumables kit, designed to make suction tamponade possible anywhere a patient needs it — not just where a wall port happens to be.

  • Battery-powered vacuum pump

    Delivers regulated negative pressure up to −250 mmHg, adjustable to accommodate dressing or seal leaks — no dependence on hospital wall suction.

  • UST consumables kit

    Single-use, sterile components sized for suction tamponade, compatible with commonly available intrauterine balloon and drainage supplies.

  • Built for low-resource and mobile settings

    Compact and rechargeable, designed to travel with the care team — labor & delivery units, ambulances, rural clinics, and disaster response.

  • Field-proven design lineage

    VATARA's pump platform has already been deployed for negative pressure wound therapy in hospitals across Cameroon, Haiti, and Ukraine — this is a mature design entering a new clinical application and market.

How It Works

From bleeding to controlled, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Place

    The care team places the intrauterine device as part of standard UST technique.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Tubing connects the device to the VATARA pump — no wall suction hookup needed.

  3. 3

    Apply Suction

    VATARA delivers steady, regulated negative pressure, helping the uterus contract and bleeding slow.

VATARA is intended for use by trained clinicians as part of a comprehensive postpartum hemorrhage protocol. Product specifications shown are for demonstration purposes as SONA Innovations prepares for the U.S. market.
